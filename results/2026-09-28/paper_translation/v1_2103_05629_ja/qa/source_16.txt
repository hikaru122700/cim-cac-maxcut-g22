such an experiment would take. This metric is conser-

heuristics for predicting when to stop the trajectories ear-
lier than Tsim could lead to faster sampling (bringing Tall
closer to Tsamp). In addition to Tall, we also study the
time Tany to sample any one of the ground or ﬁrst-excited
conﬁgurations, which is similarly deﬁned as the number
of trajectories taken to sample any one of the ground or
ﬁrst-excited conﬁgurations, multiplied by Tsim.
